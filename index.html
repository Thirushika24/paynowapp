Pay Fine - Tamil Nadu Traffic Violations

General Violations

  • Driving Without a Valid License – ₹5,000
  • Driving Without Valid Insurance – ₹2,000
  • Driving Without Registration Certificate – ₹5,000
  • Driving Without Pollution Certificate – ₹10,000
  • Disobeying Traffic Police Instructions – ₹2,000

Two-Wheeler Specific Violations

  • Riding Without a Helmet – ₹1,000 and 3-month DL suspension
  • Triple Riding – ₹2,000 and 3-month DL suspension

Four-Wheeler Specific Violations

  • Driving Without Seatbelt – ₹1,000 and/or community service
  • Overloading Passengers – ₹200 per extra passenger

Serious Offenses

  • Drunk Driving – ₹10,000 and/or 6 months imprisonment
  • Using Mobile While Driving – ₹1,000 (first offense), ₹10,000 (repeat offense)
  • Rash and Negligent Driving – ₹5,000
  • Juvenile Offenses – ₹25,000 and up to 3 years imprisonment

Other Violations

  • Overspeeding – ₹1,000 (LMV), ₹2,000 (MMV)
  • Jumping Red Light – ₹1,000 to ₹5,000
  • Obstructing Emergency Vehicles – ₹10,000
  • Driving Without Permit – ₹10,000
  • Entering No Entry Zone – ₹2,000 to ₹5,000
